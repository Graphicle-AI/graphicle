Graphicle integrates selected code and designs under one source, authorization, revision and job model. It does not bundle the complete applications below. [UPSTREAM.json](UPSTREAM.json) records pinned commits, inspected regions, hashes and implementation boundaries.

- **Microsoft GraphRAG — MIT.** Through the linked core: distinct-source community weights and token-counted report context (`../graphicle-memory/src/community.rs`) and token-counted context batching in evidence packing (`../graphicle-memory/src/packing.rs`). The graph engine's community detection uses the Rust `network_partitions` implementation from **graspologic-native 1.2.5 — MIT**, with three local lifetime annotations and algorithms unchanged; `../graphicle-graph/vendor/network_partitions/PROVENANCE.json` records the source archive and original file hashes. This service's own community reports and its global and DRIFT queries were archived before the open-source release. Microsoft and graspologic contributor notices are retained.
- **Mem0 — Apache-2.0.** Contextual extraction and memory-operation concepts. Native jobs validate source/target revisions, propose observations and require review. The managed platform, proprietary optimizations and complete SDK/provider catalogue are not included. The local comparison runs the separate pinned OSS implementation; that Python package is not bundled in the app.
- **Graphiti — Apache-2.0.** Entity/edge revisions and temporal-memory concepts, with the RRF adaptation in the core; service recall fuses its arms with RRF. Reviewed claims and source-scoped identities remain canonical in SQLite. An optional Neo4j projection supports bounded graph traversal with current-source revalidation; it is not Graphiti's complete application or Cypher/API compatibility. Graphiti's copyright notice (Copyright 2024, Zep Software, Inc.) is reproduced in `NOTICE` and in the adapted files of the linked core.
- **Hindsight — MIT.** Retain/recall/reflect design, narrative blocks, eight delta operations, dependency grounding and refresh concepts. The native source-reading loop, reviewed projections and graph links do not reproduce all upstream belief/network engines, prompts or service behavior. Optional local cross-encoder reranking blends its score with the fused rank, after `apply_combined_scoring` in `hindsight_api/engine/search/reranking.py` at commit `eb021da3b2501911e4b57c82b3de1123572a200e`; the `grounded-v2` answer contract's question period uses the core's port of that commit's `temporal_periods.py`. Optional `query_prefix` and `passage_prefix` fields in a local embedding manifest, applied to questions and indexed passages respectively, follow `encode_query`/`encode_passage` and the ONNX embedder's prefixes in `hindsight_api/engine/embeddings.py` and `embeddings_query_prefix`/`embeddings_passage_prefix` in `hindsight_api/config.py` at `0be6c02b2aafc2b6bdb188ef1842ac507e0cfa2b`. The refresh workflow's single follow-up request, which quotes refused delta operations and lists the real section and block ids, adapts `request_delta_operations` from `hindsight_api/engine/reflect/delta_ops.py` at `eb021da3b2501911e4b57c82b3de1123572a200e`, and structured replies that send an array or object property as JSON text are decoded after that commit's done-tool fix in `reflect/agent.py`. Model prompts show at most 200 characters of a source title plus `...`, after `PROMPT_CONTEXT_MAX_CHARS` and `truncate_context_for_prompt` in `hindsight_api/engine/prompt_utils.py` at `017b3f5d888d67341e70f43c102cb8155bb9f51e`, in `src/prompt_context.rs`.
- **MemOS — Apache-2.0.** Readiness, caching and scheduling designs adapted to source revisions. The core's `lifecycle.readiness` planner is called by the Python change-review runtime; this service's job queue schedules with its own leases and budgets. `model::embed_with_credential` also adapts exact within-request deduplication and ordered expansion from `CachingEmbedder.embed` at commit `a7367d07e55db61099f7b4e2c1108bc5831a24f3`; it excludes shared caches, TTLs and threaded singleflight. A selective adoption record binds that narrow change to source hashes and before/after transport tests. Native SQLite coordination and optional Redis-compatible inference dispatch are independently implemented. The default observer's preference rule adapts the user-attributed preference evidence rules of `src/memos/templates/prefer_complete_prompt.py` at the same commit (not the compromised `v2.0.34` tag). No transformer KV-cache replacement, model-weight modification or neural training is bundled.
- **Graphify — Apache-2.0 with retained MIT portions.** Source-located Tree-sitter structure extraction and ambiguity handling through the Rust core. The service adds a broader grammar pack, not every Graphify compiler feature or hosted application. Its license texts and NOTICE are retained.
- **Onyx — MIT portions only.** Chunk/source handling and selected connector pagination/document interfaces. Rust connectors use official read-only GitHub, Slack, Notion and Drive APIs, including bounded provider access refresh and recursive Drive folders. Binary document parsing uses independently licensed dependencies and the OS helper. Onyx enterprise code and the full connector catalogue are excluded. The `grounded-v2` answer contract's closing checklist adapts `ANSWER_COMPLETENESS_REMINDER` from `backend/onyx/prompts/chat_prompts.py` at commit `a7a17725f83ae34af3c498b0fd4f1a42c9c50989`. The benchmark kit's query-variant tool (`../../bench/memory/tools/expand_queries.py`, with its prompt `query_expansion_prompt.txt`) adapts the semantic-rephrase and keyword-expansion prompts of `backend/onyx/prompts/search_prompts.py`, and its weights (rephrase 1.3, keyword queries 1.0) come from `backend/onyx/tools/tool_implementations/search/constants.py`, both at commit `b2186a2dba8123ab597e3d28043b6e64619bf135`. Its hypothetical answer, which reuses that file's 0.7 for a model's uninstructed query, follows HyDE (Gao et al., 2022) and query2doc (Wang et al., 2023).
- **Mastra — Apache-2.0 (content outside `ee/`).** The default conversation observer rules in `src/observer.rs` adapt `OBSERVER_EXTRACTION_INSTRUCTIONS` from `packages/memory/src/processors/observational-memory/observer-agent.ts` at commit `381c48dbffade8285e4226c1d9443e8ff43d4b8c` (statements versus questions, state changes, dates only for datable times, precise verbs, distinguishing details of listed items). Notes keep Graphicle's exact-quote requirement. Automatic observation in `src/auto_observe.rs` follows the thresholds of `constants.ts` and `observational-memory.ts` at the same commit: an observer round once a connector's unobserved sources reach 30,000 tokens; the per-round budgets, round cap and replay checks are Graphicle's, and the asynchronous buffering is not ported. The opt-in navigable recall options (`pack_order: "chronological"`, `shown` with `already_shown`, and `skip_shown` of the MCP `recall_project` tool) in `../graphicle-memory/src/packing.rs`, `src/query.rs` and `src/api.rs` follow `searchMessagesForResource` in `packages/memory/src/tools/om-tools.ts` and `packages/memory/src/tools/om-search-context.ts` at commit `a86dc424052b8dfbbf7eadb60a9c2acadbc0abc8`, and the `reading_guidance` header line rewords the rule on user statements versus assistant suggestions in `OBSERVATION_CONTEXT_INSTRUCTIONS` (`constants.ts`) at that commit. The observed-tail fix of commit `056427cda3e9b7c064e55daaa688601807806840` needed no port, since rounds here buffer nothing; a test pins that behaviour. Copyright (c) 2025 Kepler Software, Inc.; the license is retained in `licenses/mastra-ai__mastra__LICENSE.md`.
- **Later core revisions.** Through the linked core: question-term candidates adapted from Mem0 `mem0/utils/entity_extraction.py` at `94c3fe9f238f3dbf29c9ce98643bd71eb13077cd`, English question-period rules from Hindsight `hindsight_api/engine/temporal_periods.py` at `eb021da3b2501911e4b57c82b3de1123572a200e` and section-anchor resolution from that commit's `hindsight_api/engine/reflect/delta_ops.py` (see `../graphicle-memory/UPSTREAM.json`). The opt-in `stem` option implements the published Porter (1980) algorithm. The opt-in `speaker` ranking of the user's own turns (its `"auto"` side selection from the question is Graphicle's own) follows the user-only retrieval keys in LongMemEval (MIT) `src/retrieval/run_retrieval.py` at `9e0b455f4ef0e2ab8f2e582289761153549043fc`, and the opt-in `note_keys` (observer notes keyed onto the passages their evidence quotes and merged into their lexical key) follows its key expansion in `src/retrieval/index_expansion_utils.py` at that commit; implemented independently, its license retained in `licenses/xiaowu0162__LongMemEval__LICENSE.txt`.

Additional parser grammars are pinned through **tree-sitter-language-pack 1.20.0**. `GRAMMAR_NOTICES.json` records each selected grammar, its upstream project, revision and licence, and `licenses/grammars/` holds the licence text of each of the 48 projects (the grammars are linked into the `graphicle` program). The pack's Groovy grammar is not selected, because its generated source has no clear licence grant. Groovy files are parsed by the linked graph engine's vendored `tree-sitter-groovy` 0.1.2 instead, whose author declares it MIT; `../graphicle-graph/NOTICE.md` records that basis.

`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.html` holds the licence texts of the Rust crates linked into the `graphicle` program; `cargo about` generates it from `Cargo.lock` with `about.toml`. `licenses/` holds the licence texts of the adapted upstream sources and the fonts. Where a published crate declares MIT but supplies no standalone notice, the original declaration/author information and standard SPDX text are identified explicitly in `licenses/DECLARED_CRATE_NOTICES.json`; that text is not represented as a recovered upstream copyright notice. The program also links the graph engine and the memory library; their notices and licence texts are in `../graphicle-graph/NOTICE`, `../graphicle-graph/NOTICE.md`, `../graphicle-graph/licenses/` and `../graphicle-memory/NOTICE.md`.

Model weights, provider accounts, cloud services and the upstream applications are not included in the macOS app, the Linux archives or the container image. Redis/Valkey, Neo4j and Qdrant test containers are separately downloaded optional services, not bundled executables.
